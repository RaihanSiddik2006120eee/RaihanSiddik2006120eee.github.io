Research Experience

My current research addresses generalization, frequency-domain representation, parameter-efficient adaptation, and trustworthy visual AI.

First-author manuscript in preparation

FaLoRA: Parameter-Efficient Frequency-Aware Adaptation for Generalizable Deepfake Detection

Undergraduate thesis supervised by Professor Dr. S. M. Mahbubur Rahman, Department of EEE, BUET.

I proposed a frequency-aware LoRA framework that combines DCT information, FFT phase information, and steganalytic noise filters to guide parameter-efficient adaptation of a Vision Transformer.

0.94M trainable parameters Approximately 2% trainable Cross-dataset evaluation
View research details
  • Designed the frequency-aware gating mechanism and LoRA integration.
  • Implemented the full training and evaluation pipeline in PyTorch.
  • Conducted cross-dataset evaluation without task-specific retraining.
  • Performed ablation studies and analyzed frequency-domain behavior.
  • Achieved AUC values of 90.96% on Celeb-DF v2, 79.34% on DFDC, 80.95% on WildDeepfake, and 89.26% on DeeperForensics.
Ongoing research

CLIP-LoRA for Industrial Anomaly Detection

Ongoing research supervised by Professor Dr. S. M. Mahbubur Rahman, Department of EEE, BUET.

This work investigates parameter-efficient adaptation of the CLIP visual encoder for industrial anomaly detection and defect recognition using limited labeled data.

CLIP LoRA Few-shot learning Cross-category evaluation
View research details
  • Adapting the CLIP visual encoder through low-rank updates.
  • Studying representation preservation with a small number of trainable parameters.
  • Evaluating generalization across industrial object and texture categories.
  • Preparing comparative experiments, ablation studies, and reproducible evaluation.
